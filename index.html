Your rights under Philippine law

Three laws every student should know.

Covers physical, sexual, psychological and economic abuse by a husband, ex-partner or dating partner. Survivors can seek Barangay and court protection orders. [3]

Also called the Bawal Bastos Law. It penalizes catcalling, unwanted sexual remarks, stalking and online harassment, and requires workplaces and schools to act on complaints. [4]

The comprehensive women's rights law. It commits the State to end discrimination and protect women from violence. [5]
